# Harry Potter
*The Harry Potter series is a story about a young boy named Harry who discovers he is a wizard. He goes to a magical school called Hogwarts, where he learns spells, makes friends like Ron and Hermione, and faces many adventures. Throughout the series, Harry fights against the dark wizard Voldemort, who wants to take over the wizarding world. The books show Harry growing up, learning about courage, friendship, and love. It’s a story that teaches readers about the power of kindness and standing up for what’s right.*
